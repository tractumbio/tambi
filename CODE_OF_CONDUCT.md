# Code of Conduct

> **Purpose:** Define expected behaviour for everyone participating in this project.
> **Audience:** All contributors, reviewers, and maintainers.
> **Last Updated:** 2026-08-07
> **Version:** 1.0.0
> **Author:** Adrian Cioanca (acebio)
> **Related Documents:** [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md), [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)

---

## Our pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers, pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free, professional experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, or role. This is a workplace project; the standard we hold ourselves to is at least that of a professional consulting engagement.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive in code reviews, issue discussions, and meetings.
- Assume good intent; ask clarifying questions before assuming a mistake was careless.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Credit others' contributions and ideas.
- Respect that this repository handles defence-sector and competitive-intelligence data — treat all content, even placeholder/sample data, as if it were sensitive, and never commit real client, contract, or personal data (see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)).

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, discriminatory jokes or language, personal attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without consent.
- Deliberately introducing insecure code, undisclosed data exfiltration, or bypassing review/security processes.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, reviews, or planning sessions.

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces (repository, issues, PRs, linked Azure DevOps boards, associated chat channels) and when representing the project externally.

## Enforcement

Violations should be reported to the project lead / core team maintainers listed in [README.md](README.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled confidentially. Consequences range from a private warning to removal from the project, depending on severity and recurrence.
